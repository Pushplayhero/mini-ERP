# Release Notes: `v0.3.0-suites` (Phase 3 Business Suites Milestone)

- **Version**: `0.3.0` (`v0.3.0-suites`)
- **Release Type**: Phase 3 Business Suites Milestone Release
- **Baseline Release**: `v0.2.0-platform` (`816ff40`)
- **Architecture Decisions**: [ADR-014](../adr/ADR-014-purchase-and-supplier-masterdata.md) through [ADR-017](../adr/ADR-017-mrp.md) & [Phase 3 Overview](../adr/PHASE3-suites-architecture-overview.md)
- **Database Migrations**: `0015` through `0018`

---

## 1. Overview

`v0.3.0-suites` marks the completion of **Phase 3 (Weeks 14–17)** of the `mini-erp` master plan. Building atop the hardened Phase 1 O2C accounting kernel (`v0.1.0`) and Phase 2 multi-tenant platform governance (`v0.2.0-platform`), this release delivers complete **Procure-to-Pay (`purchase` + `payables`)**, **Inventory Valuation (`costing`)**, and **Manufacturing & MRP (`mrp`)** suites—closing the end-to-end financial and physical loop (`P2P -> Costing -> Multi-Level MRP -> O2C`) with strict `import-linter` modular boundaries, `NUMERIC(20, 6)` + `ROUND_HALF_EVEN` Banker's Rounding, and fail-closed multi-tenant isolation.

## 2. Highlights by Suite Module

### 2.1 `masterdata.Supplier` & `purchase` — Procurement & Two-Stage GR/IR Accrual ([ADR-014](../adr/ADR-014-purchase-and-supplier-masterdata.md))
- **Supplier Masterdata**: Added `suppliers` under `app.modules.masterdata` (`TWD`-enforced for Phase 1–3 consistency, `CustomFieldEntityType.SUPPLIER`, `BatchEntityType.{SUPPLIER, PURCHASE_ORDER}`, and `HookContext.supplier_id`).
- **Purchase Order Lifecycle (`PO-{YYYY}-{NNNNNN}`)**: Supports `draft -> confirmed -> received` (+ `cancelled`) with confirm-time non-override `standard_cost` repricing, `purchase.order.validate_confirm` hook execution, and `WorkflowEntityType.PURCHASE_ORDER` (`confirm` / `cancel`) approval gating.
- **Physical Receipt & GR/IR Accrual**: Receiving a confirmed PO emits `purchase.goods_received`, atomically advancing `inventory` (`StockMoveType.RECEIPT`) and posting `Dr 1300 Inventory / Cr 2110 Accrued Purchases` in the same transaction.

### 2.2 `payables` — Vendor Bills, GR/IR Clearing & AP Aging ([ADR-015](../adr/ADR-015-payables.md))
- **Vendor Bills (`BILL-{YYYY}-{NNNNNN}`)**: Exact 1:1 invoicing against `RECEIVED` POs clearing GR/IR accruals (`Dr 2110 Accrued Purchases / Cr 2000 Accounts Payable`), enforced by partial unique indexes `uq_vendor_bills_order_live` and `uq_vendor_bills_supplier_invoice_no_live` plus `WorkflowEntityType.VENDOR_BILL` (`void`).
- **Outgoing Payments & R14 Allocations (`APAY-{YYYY}-{NNNNNN}`)**: Supports idempotent `request_ref` + SHA-256 `request_fingerprint` allocation commands, `WorkflowEntityType.VENDOR_PAYMENT` (`void`), and single-statement `UNION ALL` AP Aging reports (`/api/v1/payables/reports/ap-aging`).
- **Control Account Protection & Rebuild**: Expanded `KERNEL_CONTROL_ACCOUNT_CODES` with `2000` and `2110` and added operator reconciliation CLI `app.cli.rebuild_ap_balances`.

### 2.3 `costing` — Moving Weighted Average, Landed Cost & PPV ([ADR-016](../adr/ADR-016-costing.md))
- **Real-Time MWA Subledger (`product_cost_summaries` & append-only `cost_moves`)**: Subscribes deterministically to `purchase.goods_received`, `sales.goods_shipped` (with `standard_cost` zero-value fallback for legacy uncosted Phase 1 stock), and `mrp.*` movements under `inventory -> costing -> ledger.posting` ordering.
- **Cost Adjustments (`COST-{YYYY}-{NNNNNN}`)**: Supports `LANDED_COST` capitalization (`Dr 1300 / Cr 2110`), `PPV` variance clearing (`5110 Purchase Price Variance <-> 2110 Accrued Purchases`), and `REVALUATION` (`1300 <-> 5110`) with `WorkflowEntityType.COST_ADJUSTMENT` (`void`).
- **Sync, Variance Reporting & Rebuild**: Includes `/api/v1/costing/summaries/{product_id}/sync-standard-cost`, Valuation Variance reporting, and `app.cli.rebuild_cost_summaries`.

### 2.4 `mrp` — Multi-Level BOM, `1310` WIP Subledger & Net Requirements ([ADR-017](../adr/ADR-017-mrp.md))
- **Multi-Level BOM & DAG Cycle Guard**: Enforces at most one active BOM per product (`uq_boms_company_product_active`), UoM alignment, scrap-factor explosion (`/api/v1/mrp/boms/{id}/explode`), and concurrent tenant-locked DFS cycle detection rejecting cycles (`HTTP 422`).
- **Work Order Execution (`WO-{YYYY}-{NNNNNN}`) & Closed-Loop `1310` WIP**: Guards lifecycle transitions with `mrp.work_order.validate_confirm` hooks and `WorkflowEntityType.WORK_ORDER` (`confirm`/`cancel`), protects `1310 Work in Process` inside `KERNEL_CONTROL_ACCOUNT_CODES`, enforces `request_ref` + `request_fingerprint` shop-floor idempotency, protects consumed quantities on material returns, and absorbs 100% of remaining `wip_balance` upon completion / yield-loss short close.
- **Topological MRP Engine & Rebuild**: Computes Kahn-ordered multi-level net requirements (`/api/v1/mrp/planning/net-requirements`) netting on-hand stock, open POs, and open WO supply/commitments, plus `app.cli.rebuild_mrp_balances`.

### 2.5 Platform Hardening & Security Governance
- Completed Phase 2 defense-in-depth hardening (`extra="forbid"` auth schemas, `FOR UPDATE` + `populate_existing=True` session/webhook/workflow row locks, `_MAX_REGEX_LENGTH = 500`, in-flight webhook deactivation & post-HTTP settlement checks, CSV cell/key sanitization, and Act 5 integration coverage in `app.cli.demo_platform`).
- Expanded platform governance uniformly across Phase 3: **90 permissions** (`55 -> 90`), **131 classified `APIRoute`s** (`4 Public / 2 Authenticated / 125 RequiresPermission / 0 invalid`), **15** `CustomFieldEntityType` entities, **9** `WorkflowEntityType` entities (`12` transitions), and **16** `BatchEntityType` export providers.
- Re-evaluated `VULN-06` (`webhook_endpoints` signing-secret plaintext-at-rest risk) at the Phase 3 checkpoint in [threat-model.md](../security/threat-model.md), confirming all compensating controls and extending the next review checkpoint to Phase 4 / Staging production hardening.
